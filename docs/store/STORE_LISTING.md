## 2. Full Description (Google Play Store)

```markdown
Wordloom is an immersive, distraction-free English reading laboratory designed to build your vocabulary, reading cadence, and comprehension naturally through graded classic literature.

Whether you are preparing for CEFR benchmarks (A1 through C2), IELTS, TOEFL, or simply wish to rediscover timeless classics with instant dictionary assistance, Wordloom offers a thoughtfully crafted reading sanctuary.

✦ 21 GRADED CLASSIC STORIES & ANTHOLOGIES
Explore over 150 chapters and curated stories from the world’s greatest storytellers, graded across all CEFR proficiency bands:
• A1–A2 (Foundational): Aesop’s Fables, Grimm’s Fairy Tales, Sketches New & Old
• B1–B2 (Intermediate): The Canterville Ghost, Selected Stories by Anton Chekhov, The Metamorphosis, Guy de Maupassant, Edgar Allan Poe
• C1–C2 (Advanced Mastery): The Strange Case of Dr. Jekyll and Mr. Hyde, Wessex Tales, Tales from the Alhambra, Bartleby the Scrivener

✦ INSTANT WORD-TOUCH TRANSLATION & DICTIONARY
Encounter an unfamiliar word? Simply tap any word in the text to instantly reveal its contextual translation, literary definition, and grammatical part of speech in your mother tongue (Arabic, French, Spanish, German, Turkish, and more).

✦ 3 SPACED-REPETITION REVIEW GAMES
Turn words you discover while reading into permanent memory:
1. Flashcards (Recall Mode): Master definitions with adaptive spaced repetition.
2. Match It (Speed Matching): Rapidly pair English words with mother-tongue translations against the clock.
3. Type What You Hear (Listening & Spelling): Listen to authentic audio pronunciation and spell words letter-by-letter with progressive hint guides.

✦ 4 DISTINCT VOICE NARRATOR PROFILES
Customize your reading and listening experience with 4 calibrated speech archetypes:
• Natural AI Female — Warm & Melodic
• Natural AI Male — Calm & Resonant
• British Classic — Oxford Literary Cadence
• Studio Human — High-definition clarity and diction

✦ DISTRACTION-FREE PAPER EXPERIENCE
• Single-paper reading sheet with zero visual clutter
• Interactive Reading Ruler focus guide for smooth cadence
• Elegant color moods (Fern, Amber, Coral, Purple, Sky, Dark Mode)
• Custom book import (TXT and PDF)

✦ 100% PRIVATE & OFFLINE READY
Your reading journey belongs to you. Wordloom works completely offline without requiring an account or internet connection. Zero tracking, zero ads, zero data collection.

Begin your journey with Wordloom today and experience the elegance of language learning through literature.
```
