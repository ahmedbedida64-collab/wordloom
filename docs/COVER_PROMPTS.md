# Cover Style Options

## Option 1: Vintage Watercolor
**Prompt:** A classic vintage watercolor book cover illustration, muted earthy tones, delicate brushstrokes, nostalgic and timeless feel. Central focal point leaving generous negative space at the very top for typography. Clean borders, high quality, soft lighting, 4k.

## Option 2: Minimalist Flat Vector
**Prompt:** A modern minimalist flat vector book cover illustration, bold solid colors, geometric shapes, clean and crisp lines. A single striking central motif with massive empty space at the top half for the title. UI/UX style, highly aesthetic, 8k resolution.

## Option 3: Intricate Paper Cutout
**Prompt:** A beautiful layered paper cutout diorama book cover, intricate details, deep shadows and 3D depth, warm ambient lighting. A central scene crafted from layered paper, with the top third of the frame entirely clear and smooth for typography overlays. Masterpiece, high detail.
