## Strategy Change Required

The 2,420–12,100 word range requires targeting:
1. **Individual short stories** (4,000–12,000 words) — rare standalone in PG
2. **Selected chapters from novellas** — violates "complete works" rule
3. **Curated compilations of short works** — approved by Gate 1 for A1/A2

### Recommended approach:
- Use **curated short-story compilations** (approved at Gate 1):
  e.g., "Selected Aesop's Fables" = 30 fables, each as a chapter (~200 words each = 6,000 words, 50 chapters ✅)
  e.g., "O. Henry Short Stories" = select 15 stories totaling ~9,000 words ✅
  e.g., "Grimm's Selected Fairy Tales" = 25 tales totaling ~8,000 words ✅
- Use **verified short novellas**: The Yellow Wallpaper, The Awakening (28,000 — too long),
  The Turn of the Screw (42,000 — too long)

## Slots That Cannot Be Filled (Honest Gap Report)

The following slots are genuinely difficult with this word range:
- **A1-life**: Almost no unadapted A1 English texts exist at 2,400+ words
- **A1-travel**: Same problem
- **A1-history/business/technology**: Near impossible in unadapted public domain
- **A1-science**: Extremely rare
- **Technology (any level)**: PG has very few technology-theme works in this word range
