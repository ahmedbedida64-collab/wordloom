# WORDLOOM COVER STYLE GUIDE (Photo Cover Family)

## 1. Visual Identity & Photographic Philosophy
- **Medium & Style:** Fine-art atmospheric editorial photography. 35mm film aesthetic with subtle organic grain, natural soft sidelighting, and shallow depth of field.
- **Tone & Mood:** Warm, thoughtful, contemplative, timeless. Looks like a curated literary journal cover photograph.
- **Texture:** Tactile physical materials — weathered wood, aged paper, raw linen, misty landscapes, classical architecture, natural flora.

## 2. Shared Colour Palette & Topic Variations
- **Foundation Palette:** Warm almond (`#FFEBCD`), deep copper/chestnut (`#8C522A`), soft charcoal shadow (`#1C1917`), warm linen highlights (`#FAF5EF`).
- **Topic Tone Accents (Same saturation & luminance):**
  - **Life:** Olive moss green & warm amber sunlight
  - **Culture:** Dusty terracotta, parchment gold & deep indigo
  - **History:** Sepia bronze, aged stone & weathered brick
  - **Travel:** Coastal mist blue, sea salt emerald & warm dune sand

## 3. Composition Grid
- **Aspect Ratio:** 2:3 portrait (matches standard book jacket and Wordloom card frame).
- **Focal Point:** Subject positioned in the upper two-thirds or along the rule-of-thirds horizon line.
- **Negative Space:** Lower 35% of the frame is subtly darker, lower in contrast, and uncluttered so Wordloom's white title and author typography overlay is perfectly readable.
- **Lighting:** Warm low-angle natural light (golden hour or soft window light), creating soft shadows and rich specular texture.

## 4. Absolute Text Policy
- **NO TEXT:** The image must contain absolutely ZERO words, letters, logos, numbers, signatures, or synthetic watermarks. Wordloom renders all title, author, level badge, and length metadata via DOM text overlay.

## 5. Style-Lock Prompt Prefix
`Fine-art cinematic editorial photograph, 35mm film aesthetic with subtle natural grain, warm soft natural sidelighting, shallow depth of field, tactile atmospheric textures, zero text, no words, no letters:`
