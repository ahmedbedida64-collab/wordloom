# Cover Style Guide

## Visual Analysis
- **Art Style, Medium & Rendering**: Vintage watercolor and ink illustration, nostalgic, timeless. Hand-drawn aesthetic with delicate brushstrokes and visible paper texture.
- **Color Palette**: Muted earthy tones, warm sepia, deep forest green, faded gold, and parchment off-white (`#FAF4EB`, `#8B5A2B`, `#5C4033`).
- **Lighting and Mood**: Soft, diffused, ambient lighting. Mood is quiet, classic, and slightly antiquarian.
- **Composition and Framing**: Central vignette or motif, fading gracefully into the parchment background.
- **Border and Shape**: A subtle, 1.5px solid `#8B5A2B` border.
- **Top Region**: The top third of the frame is intentionally left blank/uncluttered (negative space) for text overlays.
- **Exclusions**: Never include text, letters, human faces (unless heavily stylized), modern objects, or photorealism.

## Layout & Typography Overlay
- **Title**: Centered at the top, 10% from the top edge. Font: Playfair Display, Bold, 64px, `#5C4033`.
- **Author**: Centered below title, 20% from the top edge. Font: Lora, Italic, 36px, `#8B5A2B`.
- **Level Badge**: Bottom right corner. A1/A2 (Green `#4CAF50`), B1/B2 (Blue `#2196F3`), C1/C2 (Purple `#9C27B0`).
*(Note: An open-source font matching this (e.g. PlayfairDisplay-Bold.ttf) will be placed in `assets/fonts/`)*
